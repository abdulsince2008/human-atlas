- **Known Limitations (Documented Honestly):**
  - BodyParts3D 4.0 provides an adult male reference only (`2,234` meshes, `3,432` FMA concepts); female reproductive anatomy, thyroid parenchyma, inner-ear cochlea, epicranial scalp muscles, falx/arachnoid/pia mater, upper-limb peripheral nerves, and staged embryological meshes are explicitly disclosed as conceptual/non-modeled where meshes are absent.
  - **Histology** (`asset_missing`) and **Radiology & Medical Imaging** (`asset_missing`) require openly licensed external microscopy slides and de-identified DICOM/X-ray/CT/MRI/ultrasound teaching datasets; no synthetic images are substituted as real clinical specimens or radiographs.
  - Pathophysiology presentations use healthy reference meshes with educational color highlights and explicit disclosures rather than fabricated pathological geometry.
  - All educational content remains in `draft` / `structured_reference` review states pending formal human medical-expert sign-off.
