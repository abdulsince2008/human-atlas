# Anatomy 360 — Project Status

- **Current Stage:** Connected, Searchable, Syllabus-Aware Human Sciences Knowledge Platform (`Universal Knowledge Registry v1` + `Worldwide Syllabus Navigator v1` implemented).
- **Build & Automated Check Status:** `tsc --noEmit`, `oxlint app web` (0 errors, 0 warnings), all `npm run validate:*` checks, `npm test` (`scripts/test-platform.mjs`), `npx tsx --test app/*.test.ts` (52 unit tests across 7 files), and Vite production `npm run build` (`dist/`) passing. Automated checks verify structural/referential integrity only — not medical faculty accuracy.
- **Working Platform Capabilities:**
  - **Homepage Gateway (`#/`):** Global unified search entry bar, **Section A — Find My Syllabus** (`#/syllabus`), **Section B — Browse All 16 Subjects** (`cell_biology` through `clinical_skills_public_health` with group filtering and deep links into `#/learn`), **Section C — Explore the Human Body** (12 canonical 3D systems), **Section D — Teacher & Professor Workspace** (`#/educator`) + connected labs, and live-computed registry metrics (`computeRegistryMetrics(atlas)`).
  - **Universal Knowledge & Curriculum Coverage Registry (`app/registry/`):** Canonical hierarchy (`16 Domains → 16 Subjects → 29 Chapters → 46 Registry Topics [28 anatomy + 17 biomedical + 1 planned endocrine slot]`) uniting all lessons, depth supplements, FMA 3D concepts, 32 terminology entries, 6 physiology specs, 10 histology entries, 10 clinical cases, 5 guided 3D presentations, 96 total assessment items (`43` quiz + `33` self-check + `10` clinical + `10` histology checks), 18 study resources, 12 educator guides, and 13 curriculum frameworks.
  - **Worldwide Syllabus Navigator (`#/syllabus`, `app/syllabus/`):** Browse all 13 official national/board/regulator/university frameworks with jurisdiction, URL, checked-date metadata, and explicit alignment-vs-endorsement notices — plus **"My syllabus is not listed"** local custom syllabus builder (metadata form, paste text or `.txt`/`.md`/`.csv` upload or manual topic entry, editable topic rows, synonym/keyword matcher requiring manual confirmation on `suggested` hits, multi-to-multi topic remapping, honest `available` vs `partially_available` vs `not_available_yet` coverage report, and local IndexedDB/`localStorage` persistence).
  - **Unified Educational Search (`#/search`):** Searches across all 16 domains, topics, outcomes, 3D FMA structures, terminology, physiology, histology, clinical cases, 3D walkthroughs, MCQs, official curricula, and educator guides with category/domain/track/3D filters and deduplicated results.
  - **Teacher & Professor Workspace (`#/educator` + `EducatorToolkitPanel`):** Works across all 16 domains, all 13 official frameworks, and saved custom syllabi; inspects chapter outcomes and coverage gaps; persists custom topic reorderings (`educatorSequences`); switches depth tiers (`Grades 9–12`, `BS / MBBS`, `MPhil / PhD Research`); and previews, copies, or downloads Markdown **Lecture Outlines** and **Student Worksheets** (optional answer key).
  - **3D Graphics, Guided Presentations & Interactive Labs (`#/explorer`, `#/present`, `#/physiology`, `#/histology`, `#/clinical`, `#/practical-lab`):** Merged-batch `DataTexture` PBR shader, triplanar UVs, procedural micro-detail maps, runtime LOD 0 ↔ LOD 3 switching, 10-region surgical dissection, 5 built-in 3D presentations + local WebM recording, 5 active physiology evaluators (+ 1 planned Endocrine spec), 10 clinical cases, and 10 histology entries.
- **Known Limitations (Documented Honestly):**
  - BodyParts3D 4.0 provides an adult male reference only (`2,234` meshes, `3,432` FMA concepts); female reproductive anatomy, thyroid parenchyma, inner-ear cochlea, epicranial scalp muscles, falx/arachnoid/pia mater, upper-limb peripheral nerves, and staged embryological meshes remain non-modeled or conceptual where meshes are absent.
  - **Histology** (`asset_missing`) and **Radiology & Medical Imaging** (`asset_missing`) require openly licensed external microscopy slides and de-identified X-ray/CT/MRI/ultrasound teaching datasets; no synthetic images are substituted.
  - Introductory biomedical modules across Domains B–P (1–2 topics per domain) carry `countsAsFullCoverage: false` (`introductory` / `partial`) and classify in custom syllabus checks as `partially_available` until expanded into full courses.
  - All educational content remains in `structured_reference` / `curriculum_draft` / `pending_faculty_review` states pending formal human academic faculty sign-off (zero `expert_verified` claims).
