## 5. Refunds, Chargebacks & Net Payout Rules

1. Commissions are calculated **strictly after** qualifying proceeds settle from the finalized payment provider to Company.
2. **Refund Adjustments:** If a referred customer receives a refund, 100% of the credited commission for that transaction is automatically debited (`refund_adjustment_cents`) from the Partner's pending/payable balance.
3. **Chargeback / Reversal Adjustments:** If a referred transaction is disputed or charged back, 100% of the credited commission is reversed (`chargeback_adjustment_cents`), and the entry moves to `reversed`.
4. Fraudulent, reversed, canceled-before-settlement, or unpaid orders generate **$0 commission**.

## 7. Fraud, Self-Referrals & Misleading Claims

Partner agrees **not** to:
- Purchase subscriptions through Partner's own referral link (**zero self-referrals**).
- Bid on brand-trademark keywords (`Anatomy 360`) or run deceptive coupon/cookie-stuffing extensions.
- Publish fake testimonials, guaranteed exam-pass claims (e.g., guaranteeing MDCAT or USMLE scores), medical diagnosis claims, or undisclosed promotional endorsements.

**Mandatory Creator Disclosure:** Whenever sharing an active partner link, Partner must include a clear visible disclosure, such as:
> *"Anatomy 360 Partner Disclosure: I may earn a commission if you purchase a subscription through my referral link. This does not change the price you pay."*
