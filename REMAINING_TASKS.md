# Anatomy 360 — Remaining Tasks (External Blockers & Future Roadmap)

All Release 2.2 engineering tasks (16-domain Human Biology & Medical Sciences Curriculum Hub across 5 tracks & 11 frameworks, 3D graphics/PBR/LOD pipeline, scientific integrity hardening, and Guided 3D Clinical Explainers & Video Studio) are complete and verified. Remaining items require external assets, formal clinical review, or physical device testing:

1. **External Anatomical Mesh Expansion (CC BY 4.0 / CC0):** Source and integrate license-compliant 3D meshes for adult female reproductive anatomy, paediatric anatomy, staged Carnegie-stage embryology, thyroid/parathyroid parenchyma, inner-ear cochlea/ossicles, epicranial scalp musculature, meninges (falx/arachnoid/pia), and upper-limb peripheral nerves (brachial plexus, radial, median, ulnar).
2. **Real Licensed Histological & Microbiological Micrographs:** Import verified CC BY / CC0 light-microscopy (H&E, Masson trichrome, PAS, Wright-Giemsa blood smears, Gram stains) and electron-microscopy slides with magnification and scale bars into the existing Histology and Microbiology slots (currently marked `asset_missing` / `asset_required`).
3. **Licensed De-Identified Radiology Teaching Datasets:** Source and integrate openly licensed de-identified normal X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound teaching images into the Radiology & Medical Imaging Orientation domain (currently marked `asset_missing`).
4. **Validated Quantitative Physiology Evaluators:** Implement medically reviewed mathematical parameter models for the planned Endocrine feedback simulation, ECG conduction, and acid-base Henderson–Hasselbalch sandboxes.
5. **Human Medical-Expert Review Sign-Off:** Conduct formal faculty review of anatomical notes, 16-domain biomedical modules, surgical dissection layers, clinical cases, and built-in presentations to advance provenance records beyond `draft` / `structured_reference`.
6. **Urdu Translation Pass:** Populate `.ur` fields inside `LocalizedText` objects with medically reviewed Urdu translations and RTL UI support.
7. **Physical Mobile & Cross-Browser Video Codec QA:** Test touch pinch-to-zoom, WebGL frame pacing, and `MediaRecorder` codec output (`video/webm` vs Safari `video/mp4`) on physical mobile and desktop browsers.
