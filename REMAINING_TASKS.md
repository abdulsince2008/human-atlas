# Anatomy 360 — Remaining Tasks (External Blockers & Content Expansion)

All core software engineering milestones — including the **Universal Knowledge & Curriculum Coverage Registry (`app/registry/`)**, **Worldwide Syllabus Navigator + Custom Syllabus Local Matcher (`#/syllabus`, `app/syllabus/`)**, **Unified Cross-Domain Search (`#/search`)**, **Homepage Gateway (`#/`)**, **Universal Teacher & Professor Workspace (`#/educator`)**, 3D graphics/PBR/LOD pipeline, Guided 3D Presentation Studio, and 16-domain Curriculum Hub — are implemented and verified. Remaining items require external licensed media assets, multi-year curriculum authoring, formal faculty review, or physical device testing:

1. **External Anatomical Mesh Expansion (CC BY 4.0 / CC0):** Source and integrate license-compliant 3D meshes for adult female reproductive anatomy, paediatric anatomy, staged Carnegie-stage embryology, thyroid/parathyroid parenchyma, inner-ear cochlea/ossicles, epicranial scalp musculature, meninges (falx/arachnoid/pia), and upper-limb peripheral nerves (brachial plexus, radial, median, ulnar).
2. **Real Licensed Histological & Microbiological Micrographs:** Import verified CC BY / CC0 light-microscopy (H&E, Masson trichrome, PAS, Wright-Giemsa blood smears, Gram stains) and electron-microscopy slides with magnification and scale bars into the existing Histology and Microbiology slots (currently marked `asset_missing`).
3. **Licensed De-Identified Radiology Teaching Datasets:** Source and integrate openly licensed de-identified normal X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound teaching images into the Radiology & Medical Imaging Orientation domain (currently marked `asset_missing`).
4. **Full Multi-Chapter Expansion of Biomedical Domains (B–P):** Expand introductory 1–2 module domains (Biochemistry, Pharmacology, Pathology, Microbiology, Immunology, Genetics, Embryology, Haematology, Nutrition, Clinical Skills) into multi-chapter courses so topics graduate from `partially_available` / `introductory` to full coverage.
5. **Validated Quantitative Endocrine Model:** Implement medically reviewed mathematical equations for the planned Endocrine feedback specification slot (`endocrine-feedback-planned` in `app/data/physiology-data.ts`).
6. **Human Academic Faculty Sign-Off:** Conduct formal subject-matter expert review across all 16 domains, 46 registry topics, surgical dissection layers, clinical cases, and built-in presentations to advance `reviewStatus` beyond `structured_reference` / `curriculum_draft` / `pending_faculty_review`.
7. **Urdu & Multilingual Translation Pass:** Populate `.ur` fields inside `LocalizedText` objects with medically reviewed Urdu translations and RTL layout validation.
8. **Physical Mobile & Cross-Browser Video Codec QA:** Test touch pinch-to-zoom, WebGL frame pacing, and `MediaRecorder` codec output (`video/webm` vs Safari `video/mp4`) on physical mobile and desktop devices.
