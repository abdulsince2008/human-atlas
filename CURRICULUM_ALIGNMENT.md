## 2. Pakistan Curriculum Progression (Primary Market Baseline)

1. **School Foundations (NCP General Science, Grades 4–8):**
   - Cellular organization, human organ systems (digestive, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, excretory), balanced diet and deficiency diseases, microorganisms, hygiene, and introductory immunity.
2. **Secondary / Pre-Medical (NCP Biology Grades IX–XII — Matric & FSc Pre-Medical / MDCAT):**
   - Core SLO-based domains: Nature of Biology & Classification, Cell Biology & Tissues, Biomolecules & Enzymes, Bioenergetics, Human Life Processes (Nutrition, Gaseous Exchange, Transport, Homeostasis, Support & Movement, Coordination & Control, Reproduction, Growth & Development), Genetics & Chromosomes, Acellular Life / Prokaryotes / Fungi, Immunity, and Biotechnology.
   - **Adoption Caveat:** The NCP-2026 Biology framework is **not** universally implemented across every Pakistani school. Federal/ICT (FBISE) timelines differ from provincial textbook boards (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan), where adoption remains board-dependent.
3. **Undergraduate Medical Education (PM&DC 2024 Guidelines & UHS Lahore Modular Curriculum 2K23):**
   - 5-year MBBS (**minimum 6,200 contact hours**: ~2,950 h pre-clinical/paraclinical, 1,700 h Medicine & Allied, 1,625 h Surgery & Allied, plus longitudinal/elective/self-directed balance allocated by institutions) plus 1-year compulsory house job/internship.
   - **UHS Modular 2K23** organizes years into horizontally and vertically integrated organ-system modules (Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Nervous, Renal, GI/Hepatobiliary, Musculoskeletal, Endocrine/Reproductive) paired with OSCE/OSPE and skills labs.

## 3. International Comparisons

- **India — CBSE Biology Classes XI–XII (2026–27):**
  - Class XI: Diversity, Structural Organisation in Animals, Cell Structure & Function (Cell, Biomolecules, Cell Cycle), Plant Physiology, **Human Physiology** (Breathing, Body Fluids/Circulation, Excretion, Locomotion, Neural Control, Chemical Coordination).
  - Class XII: **Human Reproduction & Reproductive Health**, **Genetics & Molecular Basis of Inheritance**, **Human Health & Disease (Pathogens, Immunity, Cancer, Drug Abuse)**, Biotechnology, Ecology.
- **England — National Curriculum Science (KS3 & KS4):**
  - Strictly applies to maintained schools in **England**. Covers Cell Biology, Organisation (digestive, circulatory, respiratory), Infection & Response, Bioenergetics, Homeostasis & Response, Inheritance/Variation, and Working Scientifically.
- **United States — AP Biology (College Board):**
  - Advanced elective (not a universal US high-school syllabus). Strong depth in Chemistry of Life, Cell Structure & Transport, Cellular Energetics, Cell Communication & Cell Cycle, Heredity, and Gene Expression/Regulation (with minimal gross human anatomy).
- **China — MOE Compulsory Education Biology Standards (2022, Grades 7–9):**
  - Emphasizes scientific literacy across Cell Biology, Human Body Structure & Physiology basics, Heredity & Variation, Infectious Disease Prevention, and Healthy Living.
- **Australia — Western Australia Senior Secondary Human Biology (ATAR Units 1–4):**
  - State-specific course dedicated specifically to human biology: Year 11 (Cells, Tissues, Organ Systems, Reproduction, Heredity) and Year 12 (Homeostasis, Nervous & Endocrine Control, Metabolic Regulation, Body Defences & Immunity).
- **Representative Medical Universities (Not Global Standards):**
  - **Harvard Medical School (Pathways):** Integrated pre-clerkship blocks (*Foundations*, *Homeostasis*, *Immunity in Defense & Disease*, *Mind, Brain and Behavior*, *Practice of Medicine*) transitioning early into the Principal Clinical Experience (PCE).
  - **University of Oxford (Pre-Clinical A100):** Traditional split pre-clinical Years 1–3 (*Organisation of the Body*, *Physiology & Pharmacology*, *Biochemistry & Medical Genetics*, *Applied Physiology*, *Principles of Pathology*, *Medical Sociology*) awarding a BA in Medical Sciences before clinical school.

## 4. Shared Core vs Country-Specific & Out-of-Scope Topics

- **Shared Human-Biology Core (appears across nearly all school/pre-med frameworks):** Cell ultrastructure & membrane transport, mitosis/meiosis, major human organ systems, nutrition & digestion, respiration & circulation, homeostasis, DNA/central dogma & inheritance, infectious pathogens & vaccination/immunity.
- **Shared Medical-Foundation Core (PM&DC, UHS, Harvard, Oxford):** Gross/regional/neuroanatomy, histology, embryology, organ physiology, medical biochemistry, genetics, microbiology, immunology, general & systemic pathology, pharmacology, haematology/lab medicine, clinical reasoning, ethics, and epidemiology.
- **Country-Specific / Institution-Specific Variations:** PM&DC/UHS explicit Forensic Medicine & Community Medicine hour allocations; WA ATAR dedicated Human Biology track; AP Biology's 25% lab-inquiry mandate; Oxford's 3-year BA Honours research year vs Harvard's early PCE clerkship transition.
- **Out of Scope for ANATOMY 360:** Plant physiology, botany, agricultural biotechnology, ecosystem/biodiversity ecology (outside human infectious/environmental health), veterinary anatomy, patient-specific clinical diagnosis, individualized drug prescribing/dosing, and accredited degree/OSCE certification.
