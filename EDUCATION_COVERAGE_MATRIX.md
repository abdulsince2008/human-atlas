## Status Legend (Used Strictly & Consistently)
- `implemented_validated` — Real instructional content, working interactive visual/3D/simulation, assessment, curriculum mapping, and passing automated tests.
- `implemented_partial` — Real instructional content, conceptual visuals/diagrams, curriculum mapping, and self-checks implemented, with documented depth or secondary-asset gaps.
- `content_only_no_visual` — Textual explanation only without interactive/visual support.
- `asset_missing` — Educational structure and text implemented, but genuine licensed external media (microscopy slides, medical radiographs/DICOM, or missing 3D anatomy) is required before claiming full visual implementation.
- `planned_scaffold_only` — Specification or UI slot exists without a validated working model.
- `not_present` — Not yet implemented in the repository.
- `out_of_scope` — Deliberately outside ANATOMY 360’s human biology & medical education mission.
